A voice-first AI gym coach, built from scratch.

Talk to it between sets. It logs what you say, adapts your plan from your own data, remembers you, and is stopped by a safety layer when it should be.

See how it works View the code

Plain Python and TypeScript. No agent framework and no trained models: numbers come from deterministic tools, and the language model only picks tools and words the reply.

  • 1.0 smedian time to the first spoken word

Measured on this project's replay harness with scripted and synthetic speech, not on a crowd of real users. The first real gym sessions found real bugs; they are listed below, honestly.

What it does

  • Talks between sets

    Say "sixty kilos, five reps, done" or the Hinglish version. It answers in under two seconds, short, and you can cut it off.

  • Logs from speech

    Understands corrections ("sixty, no, sixty-five"), asks for a missing number instead of guessing, and never logs a number you did not say.

  • Adapts your plan

    Small signals make small nudges, strong persistent ones make bigger changes, and pain acts immediately. Every change is written to a ledger with its reason.

  • Remembers you

    Beliefs with uncertainty, patterns found in your own data and tested statistically, and forecasts that score themselves.

  • Has a safety layer

    A critic with veto power and a policy gate in code: calorie floor, load cap, no volume increase under pain.

  • Keeps you in charge

    Autonomy dial, veto windows, approvals, a kill switch, and a "why did you change this?" answer for every decision.

The whole system

Where everything runs and which way the data flows. The phone talks to your own PC through a free tunnel. The PC runs one isolated container per person, a gateway, and a GPU voice agent.

  • Isolated per person

    Each person has their own container, volume and token. The gateway only routes, and every container still checks its own token, so a gateway bug cannot cross users.

  • The voice agent holds no key and no data

    It does audio only and calls the person's backend over HTTP. The only thing that leaves the PC is text prompts to the model API.

  • Degrades instead of failing

    No voice agent: the app uses the phone's recogniser. No model: a rule-based brain answers. A dead model name: the fallback chain skips it.

One spoken turn, end to end

What happens between you finishing a sentence and the coach speaking, including interruptions. The audio is streamed continuously, so the coach starts working before the turn model has even confirmed you are done.

  • Streams everything

    Audio in, model output and speech out are all streamed. The first sentence is spoken while the rest is still being written.

  • Starts early

    The model call begins at the end of speech, not after the turn model confirms it, and is cancelled if you keep talking.

  • Can be interrupted

    It stops in about 0.3 s, and its memory keeps only the part you actually heard.

  • Tells "yeah" from "wait"

    If the interruption was only a backchannel or a cough, it carries on from the start of the sentence it was cut in.

The brain, the safety layer and the memory

How a reply is decided and kept safe, how background agents change the plan, and how the system gets to know a person.

What is enforced in code, not in prompts

  • Agent scope

    Nutrition cannot move a workout. Specialists only return typed proposals and cannot write state.

  • Safety limits

    Calorie floor, a 10% weekly load cap, volume within 50 to 125% of baseline, and no volume increase under pain.

  • Numbers

    A spoken sentence may only contain numbers from the state, a tool result or your own words. The check runs on every sentence.

  • Skips

    "We are not skipping anything" never skips. A vague "let's move on" asks first. Naming an exercise that is not in today's plan skips nothing.

The agent team

  • TrainingVolume changes, swaps, restrictions and new blocks from your lift trends and readiness.
  • NutritionCalorie and macro changes from the weight trend and your goal.
  • RecoveryScores readiness from sleep, soreness and energy, then trims volume or escalates.
  • PeriodizationDeloads, tapers and where you are in the block.
  • Progress analystFinds plateaus and forecasts; writes findings back to memory.
  • LogisticsReads your calendar: travel and busy days move or shorten sessions.
  • FormReads pose-keypoint data for hedged technique cues. Never diagnoses.
  • ResearchAnswers evidence questions from a local library and says "no sources" rather than guess.
  • IntakeRuns the onboarding interview and turns answers into validated values.
  • Safety criticApproves, revises or blocks proposals. Has veto power. If it cannot run, it fails closed: only safer or smaller changes go through.

How it was tested

A replay harness runs scripted conversations (and, in audio mode, real speech recognition and synthesis) against a throwaway copy of the backend and prints a scorecard. Every sentence from the first real-phone failures was added to it as a regression test.

  • Audio mode

    English word error rate 2.0% on the lines it transcribed, first spoken word about 1.0 s, barge-in stop about 0.3 s. Hindi and Hinglish lines are scored apart because Roman spelling varies.

  • Safety red-team

    The Agent OS has golden scenarios and a red-team set that run through the real loop and are judged on invariants of the final state, not on wording.

  • About 1,600 tests

    Backend, Agent OS, voice agent and app, plus a 12-week simulated lifter that checks the adaptation logic against a control that never adapts.

The scenarios and the lifter are hand-written. They check that the logic behaves as designed, not that it works for every real person.

What the gym taught us

The replay did not find these. Real sessions on a real phone did, and they are tracked in the error ledger in the repo.

  • FixedNothing was heard at the gym. The browser voice page trusted the sample rate the browser reported, and with a headset route the microphone delivered twice as many samples per second. Whisper heard sped-up noise and returned nothing. The page now measures the real rate in the first second. The next session was heard clearly.
  • FixedWhole sentences silently lost. A library rule dropped any speech segment followed by silence. A pause inside "We are not skipping anything... we are doing every exercise" lost the first half.
  • Fixed"We are not skipping anything" skipped an exercise. Skips are now clause-aware and checked in code before they run.
  • WorkaroundEarphones were ignored. The app's audio module forces the loudspeaker. The browser voice page plays through earphones; the native fix is waiting for the next app build.
  • OpenSeveral sets in one sentence ("50, 60 and 70 for 15") log only the last one.
  • OpenNo "undo last set" by voice, and no way to log an exercise that is not in today's plan.
  • OpenEnglish mistaken for Hinglish when a sentence repeats a short word such as "do", so the coach answers in Hindi.
  • OpenA set can be logged twice when a long sentence is split across two turns.
